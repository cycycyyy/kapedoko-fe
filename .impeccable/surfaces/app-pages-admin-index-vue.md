## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. The admin dashboard is the desk glance: what to publish, what to verify, which reports to pick up, and a scan of live promotions and people.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

KapeDoko admins deciding what to do next, on a laptop or a phone. Read the real counts, see empty queues as settled, and open moderation for a photo or a review. Bag-label world stays. Menu stays. Shared shell and rails are not restyled for other screens.

## Direction and memorable moment

A clear queue wears a teal check on stock, like a bag already stamped and set aside. A count that needs work wears the orange desk stamp. An open report is a slip you pick up — the review quote is the reported line, and Open moderation is the orange action.

## Unresolved decisions

Moderation itself stays on /admin/moderation. Publish and claim queues keep their existing ticket links.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is a tally label over settled queues and report slips, refusing a run-on stat sentence, a hero metric, and a text link as the only way into moderation.

OWN-WORLD: Shelf #F2F2F2, stock #FAF8F5, ink #1c1917, teal chrome, orange action fills. Kumbh Sans. 16px label corners, hairline borders, no lift shadows.

STORY: The admin sees which counts are quiet, which totals are live, and which queues need a person, then opens moderation on the photo or the review that is actually waiting.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Menu and Dashboard heading stay. Eight tally beats on one stock label. Two queue strips. Open reports as separate photo and review slips with an orange Open moderation control.

FORM: Desk glance, delight on the incumbent admin desk. Signature interaction: a non-zero pending, claims, or reports count sits on the orange stamp; an empty queue states the clear line beside a teal check.
