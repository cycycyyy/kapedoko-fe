# Add a cafe

Visitor mode: Operate
Audience: People who found a work-ready cafe in the Philippines and want it on the map.
Job: Submit a pending listing for admin review.
Primary action: Continue through Name → Pin → Hours → Check, then Submit cafe.
Constraints: Same fields; pending until approved; no WiFi/outlets; stepped wizard; full-screen pin; not a campaign landing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Add a cafe is a bag-label form on the cool shelf — ink on matte stock, orange Continue, teal focus — not a rice-paper sheet with lift shadows.
OWN-WORLD: Shelf field #F2F2F2, matte stock #FAF8F5, Kumbh Sans, 16px label corners (8px logo slot), 1px ink-mix hairline, no drop shadows. Orange fill + ink text on the sticky Continue. Three short orange ticks, not pills.
STORY: A contributor names a cafe they found, pins it in the Philippines, adds hours, checks the pending listing, and submits it. It stays off Home and Map until approved. Pins outside the Philippines are blocked.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Cool shelf: back, Add a cafe, three ticks. Stock label with “What’s the cafe called?”, 44px name field, 96px logo stamp. Continue pinned above the home indicator.
FORM: Map overlay; pin step collapses the sheet to a search strip over a full-bleed map, matching Map’s hairline search island.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
