## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. Cafe requests is the publish stamp queue on the admin desk.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

KapeDoko admins at a desk, laptop or phone. Scan pending listings by name and street, stamp one live, write a rejection, or stamp the whole crate. Bag-label world stays; no window.prompt; no new identity.

## Direction and memorable moment

Each request is a stock ticket. Approve lands as an orange stamp. The crate dock can stamp every pending cafe at once.

## Unresolved decisions

Cafe edit stays on /admin/cafes/[id]. Ownership claims stay on their own queue.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Cafe requests is a stamp queue of name-and-street tickets, refusing stacked generic cards, prompt dialogs, and coordinates as the scan path.

OWN-WORLD: Shelf #F2F2F2, stock #FAF8F5, ink #1c1917, teal chrome, orange action fills. Kumbh Sans. 16px label corners, hairline borders, no lift shadows.

STORY: The admin finds a pending cafe by name or address, stamps Approve or writes a rejection in place, or stamps every pending ticket from the dock.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Stock nav, teal on Requests. Find field. Status chips with counts. Pending tickets: name, street, compact hours, orange Approve and ghost Reject. Sticky dock: Approve all and Reject all.

FORM: Stamp crate, local extension of the admin desk. Signature interaction: the Approve stamp seats at a slight rotate; bulk confirm happens in the dock, never a modal.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
