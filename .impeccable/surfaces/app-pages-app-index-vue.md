## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. Home, with the shared header and menu, proves the bag-label identity.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Metro Manila workers and students choosing a cafe they can work from. Search, filter, and open a shop. WiFi, outlets, hours, and open or closed stay in words. The name stays kapé DOKO.

## Direction and memorable moment

Each cafe is a bag on a shelf. The label carries the name, the place, and three fact lines.

## Unresolved decisions

Map, Saved, and Profile keep their layouts and inherit the bag colors. Their own compositions come later.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Home is a shelf of coffee bags, each a label of name, origin, and three fact lines, refusing photo cards and the route plate.

OWN-WORLD: Matte stock #f6f1e8, ink #1c1917, one coffee brown #6f4e37. Satoshi, not condensed caps. One-color print.

STORY: The visitor reads whether a cafe is open and whether WiFi and outlets are known, then opens one, searches, or filters.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark on the stock. Search under it. Filters as printed tags. Stacked labels: large name, district, then hours, WiFi, and outlets in words. Closed is one small line under the name. The menu is four quiet words, the active one in coffee brown.

FORM: Coffee bag label, first of the safer coffee-shop list, seed a116fe1f. Signature interaction: a filter change reprints the fact lines with a short settle of the paper.
