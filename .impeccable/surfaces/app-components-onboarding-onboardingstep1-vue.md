# Onboarding amenity lesson

## Scope and visitor mode

Operate. First-run step one. Job is to show that WiFi and outlets live as stamps on each bag label, then let them go Next.

## Audience, job, action, constraints

Someone new to the app, on a phone in the Philippines. Constraints: inherit bag-label world; match Home card anatomy (logo slot, name, origin, tilted stamps); do not invent a cafe, hours, or ratings; keep Skip, ticks, Next, and the existing lesson sentence.

## Direction and memorable moment

A teaching bag that is a Home label. Orange Yes stamps press onto the stock, same stamps they will scan later.

## Unresolved decisions

None. Copy stays the existing WiFi and outlets sentence.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The amenity lesson is a bag label whose WiFi and Outlets stamps press Yes — not a two-line key/value diagram.

OWN-WORLD: Cool shelf, 16px stock bag, 8px logo slot, ink hairline, Kumbh Sans, orange stamp wash, paper tilt.

STORY: They see the stamps land, read that icons mark WiFi and outlets, and tap Next.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark, Skip. Teaching bag with app mark, “On each cafe,” two Yes stamps. Lesson sentence, ticks, Next.

FORM: Local extension of onboarding; Home bag card as topology. Code-led.
