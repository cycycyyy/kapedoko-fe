# Add a cafe

Visitor mode: Operate
Audience: People who found a work-ready cafe in Metro Manila and want it on the map.
Job: Submit a pending listing for admin review.
Primary action: Continue through Name → Pin → Hours → Check, then Submit cafe.
Constraints: Same fields; pending until approved; no WiFi/outlets; stepped wizard; full-screen pin; not a campaign landing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Add a cafe is a Map overlay you fill — rice-paper ground, floating white sheet, sticky coffee-brown Continue — not a coffee-brown campaign hero over a stacked form.
OWN-WORLD: Coffee-brown chrome on rice-paper, compact Satoshi, 8px white sheets with 0 2px 8px shadow, 50px fields, 45/48px CTAs, three coffee-brown ticks.
STORY: A contributor names a cafe they found, pins it in Metro Manila, adds hours, checks the pending listing, and submits it. It stays off Home and Map until approved. Pins outside Metro Manila are blocked.
FIRST VIEWPORT: White fade: back, Add a cafe, three ticks. White sheet with “What’s the cafe called?”, 50px name field, 96px logo stamp. Continue pinned above the home indicator.
